About me

Creative, proud, warm-hearted and full of rhythm.

Red Bonita is a girl with many beautiful passions: photography, writing, video editing, music, dancing, cooking and baking. She writes everything from song lyrics and short poetic texts to her own stories, and she edits her videos herself with a feeling for detail that is more than just talent.

Her proud heritage carries a fire that feels truly Latina — a fire she lets shine when she dances until morning. In the kitchen she is just as alive: Mexican food feels like home to her, and when she cooks or bakes, it feels like every dish has a chance to become something special.

But music can never be missing. The mix of warm kitchen aromas and her beautiful singing can draw friends, family or even neighbors closer to share a plate and a moment. That is exactly what matters to her: bringing people together. Family comes first, and beside that family are her small loyal dog companions, who are part of her heart too.
