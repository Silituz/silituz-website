About Silituz

Creative visions

Hi, I’m Silituz 👋

I bring ideas to life – with images, music and small interactive tools. Anime aesthetics meet modern AI and community vibes.

The core of it all: “There are no problems, only solutions.” I love to experiment playfully and make things easy for others to use.

On TikTok, YouTube & co. I share visuals, hooks and experiments around my green snake Silituz – sometimes funny and cute, sometimes epic with bass and shine.

What inspires me

Contrasts, clear compositions, strong eyes – and colors full of energy (green is my thing). AI for me is a tool to make ideas visible and tangible faster.

What I do

  • Illustrations & short video edits
  • Music & lyrics (hooks, themes, drops)
  • Mini apps & generators for the web
